1. PROJECT OVERVIEW  
The Zico Platform is a multi‑role digital service distribution system that allows 
administrators and agents to sell digital services including data bundles, boosting 
services, result checkers, and AFA registrations.  
The platform operates through a hierarchical admin‑agent structure allowing 
administrators to manage agents, track sales performance, process transactions, and 
distribute services through automated APIs or manual fulfillment systems.  
The system is designed to support scalable agent networks, automated role upgrades, and 
performance‑based incentives.  
Key services include:  
 Data Bundles  
 Boosting Services  
 Result Checkers  
 AFA Registrations

4. ADMIN ROLE LEVELS  
4.1 Admin (Default Level)  
Every user who signs up automatically becomes an Admin.  
Capabilities:  
 Recruit and manage agents  
 Receive st andard product prices from suppliers  
 Sell services through the platform  
Qualification: Automatic role assigned upon signup.  
4.2 Super Admin  
Super Admins receive discounted product pricing and higher profit margins.  
Benefits:  
 Reduced service pricing  
 Higher profit potential  
Qualification Requirements:  
 Must be active for at least 3 months  
 Must have at least 30 agents  
 Average daily sales of at least GHS 500
